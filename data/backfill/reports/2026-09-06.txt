Method
The authors derive a finite-memory optimal stopping rule based on a truncated Shiryaev–Roberts
statistic that aggregates likelihood-ratio evidence for all candidate onset times over the previous ξ
observations.
False alarms are controlled through an average run length constraint.
arXiv

your residual signal after an orbital maneuver is not permanently shifted because the predictor rapidly
adapts or because subsequent OD updates absorb the maneuver.

likelihood model.
